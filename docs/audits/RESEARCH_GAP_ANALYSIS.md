# Research gap analysis

This is a source-to-mechanism review. It cites primary papers, first-party engineering reports or specifications. It does not transfer published effect sizes to AAES. Every skill's bundled research basis and technical guide record the implementation boundary.

| Skill | Source and evidence used | AAES fidelity and open gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| skill-orchestrator | [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification), format and disclosure; [OpenAI skill evaluation](https://developers.openai.com/blog/eval-skills), trace-based assessment | Follows portable format. Structured ranking is an AAES policy; no proof of native semantic activation. |
| research-orchestrator | [DeepResearch Bench (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11763), report quality and citation support are separate | Ledger validates provenance links and dependence groups. Independent retrieval and entailment remain host/reviewer work. |
| adaptive-reasoning | [Snell et al. (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.03314), compute allocation depends on task difficulty | Budgeted external decisions are an adaptation. No control of a closed model's internal compute and no reproduced paper result. |
| process-verifier | [Lightman et al., Let's Verify Step by Step (2023)](https://cdn.openai.com/improving-mathematical-reasoning-with-process-supervision/Lets_Verify_Step_by_Step.pdf), intermediate supervision; [Anthropic agent evals (2026)](https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents), independent outcomes | Tests acceptance gates against recorded observations. No learned reward model or source authentication. |
| trajectory-analyzer | [OpenTelemetry trace concepts](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/concepts/signals/traces/), context, parent spans and timing | Consumes a normalized export; sampling, cross-host clock correction and causal validation require external data. |
| long-horizon-executor | [Temporal Event History](https://docs.temporal.io/encyclopedia/event-history), durable replay; [METR (2025)](https://metr.org/blog/2025-03-19-measuring-ai-ability-to-complete-long-tasks/), task completion by duration | Local durable journal only. A task-completion horizon and storage durability are different measurements; distributed recovery remains unverified. |
| multi-agent-router | [Anthropic, Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents), parallel workflows and orchestrator-workers | Resource exclusions and cost model are executable. Host dispatch, consolidation fidelity and real speedup are unverified. |
| evolutionary-optimizer | [Google DeepMind, AlphaEvolve (2025)](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/), evaluator-guided program search | Bounded Pareto selection on measured variants. No claim of autonomous generation, discovered algorithms or reproducing published gains. |
| runtime-guardian | [Greenblatt et al. (2023/2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06942), evaluating control protocols under adversarial behavior | Local policy and atomic reservation; a real, exclusively credentialed executor is necessary for an enforcement guarantee. |
| hypothesis-lab | [Gottweis et al. (2025/2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18864), proposal and critique | Independent evidence groups and descriptive ranking. No replication of the paper's agent system, tournament or scientific results. |
| experiment-evaluator | [Anthropic agent evals (2026)](https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents), trials, graders and outcomes | Paired descriptive gates and optional task-cluster resampling. Coverage depends on independent representative clusters; a small sample remains weak. |
| mechanistic-interpretability | [Zhang and Nanda (2023/2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042), patch metrics and corruption sensitivity | Toy hidden-activation intervention and metric tests only. Weight-level model results and circuit generalization remain unverified. |
| agentic-rl-lab | [Luo et al., Agent Lightning (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.03680), execution/training separation and transitions | Validates transitions and scores returns offline; no causal credit model, training, or demonstrated generalization. |
