# Research basis

Primary reference: [Anthropic, Demystifying evals for AI agents (2026)](https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents).

Outcome-based evaluation separates tasks, trials, graders, transcripts and real state. AAES computes finite-trial pass@k and pass^k and exports review records; it does not invoke external judges or assert generalization.
