# Research basis

Primary reference: [Research plans and evidence synthesis](https://openai.com/index/introducing-deep-research/).

OpenAI describes research as multistep discovery and synthesis across sources. AAES applies this as a human-auditable evidence ledger and an experiment plan, with host-provided tools only. The product's reported outcomes do not transfer to this skill; there is no browser implementation or benchmark claim.

## Applicability and failure boundary

[DeepResearch Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11763) distinguishes report quality from citation support. The offline checker validates the evidence graph and preserves contrary groups; it does not replicate the benchmark, judge citation accuracy or establish the truth of the claim.
