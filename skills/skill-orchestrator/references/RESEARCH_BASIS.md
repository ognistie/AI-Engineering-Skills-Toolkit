# Research basis

Primary reference: [Agent Skills format and progressive disclosure](https://agentskills.io/specification).

The specification defines discovery and filesystem conventions; it does not specify a ranking algorithm. AAES adds a portable semantic capability registry and a deterministic gate after host interpretation. The spec is evidence of a format contract, not of selection accuracy.

## Applicability and failure boundary

The [OpenAI skill evaluation method](https://developers.openai.com/blog/eval-skills) separates behavior observed in traces from final-text claims. Policy scores after classification are useful regression evidence and cannot establish native activation precision.
