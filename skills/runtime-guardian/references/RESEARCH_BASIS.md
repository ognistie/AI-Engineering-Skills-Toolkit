# Research basis

Primary source: [Greenblatt et al., AI Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06942).

AI Control evaluates system-level protocols under adversarial model behavior. AAES applies a narrower principle: hard checks run outside the proposing model. Its helper simulates policy decisions and does not enforce a real host, establish identity or reproduce control guarantees.

## Applicability and failure boundary

A preflight allow result is not a credential or barrier. The local reservation prevents duplicate claims inside its SQLite transaction; only a wrapper with exclusive credentials and independent audit can enforce external effects.
