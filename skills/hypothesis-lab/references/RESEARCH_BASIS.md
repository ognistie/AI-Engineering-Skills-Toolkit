# Research basis

Primary source: [Google, Towards an AI co-scientist](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18864).

The research system proposes and critiques scientific hypotheses through multiple mechanisms. AAES uses a narrower offline evidence matrix and a falsification workflow. It does not implement tournament search, simulate scientific debate, or calculate posterior probabilities.

## Applicability and failure boundary

Tournament or consensus mechanisms do not constitute independent evidence. AAES sorts observations descriptively and requires a discriminating intervention before strengthening a causal claim.
