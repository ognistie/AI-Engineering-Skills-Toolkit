# Research basis

Primary reference: [Manus, Context Engineering for AI Agents (2025)](https://manus.im/en/blog/Context-Engineering-for-AI-Agents-Lessons-from-Building-Manus).

Manus describes retaining restorable artifacts and error signals outside the prompt. The helper compares checkpoint fields and optionally verifies local artifact bytes. It is not transactional storage or authentication.
